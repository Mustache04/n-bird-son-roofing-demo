The story so far

It started with one van and a standard worth protecting.

N. Bird & Son began as a small local roofing business in 1987. The company has grown, but the work is still built around the same fundamentals: turn up, explain the job properly and leave a finish the family name can stand behind.

Today, experienced roofing teams handle repairs, complete roof replacements, flat roofing and specialist details across Solihull, Birmingham, Warwickshire and the surrounding region. Family management keeps responsibility visible from the first call to the final check.
