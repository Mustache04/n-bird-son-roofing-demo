From repair to replacement

The right solution starts with an honest assessment.

Every roof is different. The team inspects the detail, explains the options in plain English and recommends work that fits the condition of the property rather than forcing every enquiry into the same package.

01 / New roofs

A complete roof, built as one system.

Full replacement roofs in tile or slate, coordinated from the initial strip-off through structure, ventilation, underlay, battens, coverings and final weathering details.

  • Pitched tiled and slate roofs
  • Replacement tiled and slate roofs
  • Dry ridge and dry verge systems
  • Scaffolding and waste coordinated
View completed roofs
02 / Repairs

Find the cause. Fix the detail.

A leak can travel before it becomes visible indoors. Repairs focus on identifying the source, documenting what is found and restoring the roof without creating unnecessary work.

  • Leaks and slipped or broken tiles
  • Storm and impact damage
  • Valleys, ridges and mortar repairs
  • Insurance-related roofing work
Tell us what has happened
03 / Flat roofing

Flat roofs designed to drain and endure.

Replacement and new flat-roof systems for garages, extensions, porches and dormers, with careful attention to falls, outlets, upstands and adjoining walls.

  • Felt and GRP systems
  • Flat roof repairs and renewals
  • Skylight installation
  • Flat-to-pitched conversions

Common questions

Useful answers before a site visit.

Still unsure what you need? A short phone conversation is often enough to point you in the right direction.

Speak to the office
Do you provide free quotations?

Yes. The team can arrange the appropriate survey and provide a written, no-obligation quotation for the agreed scope.

Can you help with a small repair?

Yes. Small repairs, slipped tiles, mortar issues and isolated leaks are part of the service, subject to access and availability.

Is the work guaranteed?

Qualifying roofing work is covered by a 10-year guarantee. The applicable terms are confirmed with the quotation.

Which areas do you cover?

The team works across Solihull, Birmingham, Coventry, Warwickshire and the wider West Midlands. Call with your postcode to confirm availability.
